Closed-loop sensitivity

Move the car half a metre. Watch the plan change.

Each run replays the logged ego through the same warm-up, displaces it once on the frame the policy takes over, and hands over from there. Same scenario, same traffic, same reconstruction, same seed — so whatever changes between two positions of the slider is the policy's sensitivity to that pose, and nothing else.

Seed 0, recon camera rig, LQR tracker, semi-reactive traffic, from /avl-west/runs/20260905-handoff-perturb-demo. Two of the sweep's 54 scenarios are shown here. Every displacement was screened before it was simulated — the moved ego had to stay on the drivable surface and keep 0.3 m from every other actor. The sweep is still running, so a struck-through tick is a displacement that has not finished evaluating; the slider skips those rather than showing a hole.
