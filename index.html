Leaf badges are the scenario allocation against the ≥ 10-per-leaf target: 27 of 28 leaves reach it — 270 scenarios allocated, each credited to exactly one leaf. 19 leaves fill from the reviewed nuPlan mining pool (417 scenarios reviewed, 178 qualify at Keep = Y; assignment solved as an exact bipartite allocation maximizing leaves at target). Eight leaves with no usable pool — C-7 C-10 V-10 V-11 R-2 R-3 R-4 I-3 — take their membership from the 81-scenario for-editing queue built by scene editing; C-9 has no nuPlan precursor at all and its ten come by escalation from V-4 / C-6 review. V-8 Illegal U-Turn has no candidate that survives review and stands at 0 / 10. Dashed leaves have no nuPlan scenario type, so no tag reaches them; C-8 is context-only — on_carpark supplies the setting, not the event. Sources: NavSafe_Scenario_Mining_Status_full_v2 (navsafe_scenario_review-8.xlsx, reconciled); taxonomy structure: docs/research/Navsafe_taxonomy_short.md §§1–4, §8.2.

Counts are scenarios, not frames: a type hit on several frames of one scene counts once, and a scene can hold several types — so the numbers overlap and never sum to 421. That is why leaf tooltips and categories report how many of their types occur rather than a scene total.
